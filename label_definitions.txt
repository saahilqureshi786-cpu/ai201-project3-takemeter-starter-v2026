# TakeMeter label definitions
#
# These definitions match the taxonomy documented in README.md.
# baseline.py reads this file.

analysis: a post that makes a basketball claim and supports it with a specific statistic, checkable fact, comparison, example, or concrete chain of reasoning
hot_take: a post that confidently states an opinion, prediction, ranking, or conclusion without providing meaningful evidence or reasoning to support the claim
reaction: a post whose main purpose is an immediate emotional response to a game, player performance, trade, highlight, injury, announcement, or other recent basketball event rather than an argument
question: a post whose main purpose is to ask the community for an explanation, comparison, prediction, recommendation, or opinion instead of primarily asserting its own conclusion
